Paradox Ironmon
The concept of Paradox is from the Pokemon Scarlet/Violet reimagining older pokemon as brand new pokemon with new types. The idea of the challenge is for runs to feel even more unique from another and reward the player for adapting to the information given. The challenge will be most similar to kaizo ironmon but the difficulty will be in between that and super kaizo, both challenges made by iateyourpie.
The ParaDex
Each seed in Paradox randomizes all Pokemon into dual types with updates to match visually. The changes also adds new challenges to the game:
  • Each type are distributed evenly, with ~77 mons per type per seed
  • Ghost, Dark, Steel, and Dragon are more common, making types they are strong into worse
Adjusted Evolutions
Pokemon evolutions are adjusted in Paradox in a few ways:
All Pokemon in a species line will share the same dual typing, i.e., if Nincada is a Dark/Ghost, so are Ninjask and Shedinja.
The only exceptions are Pokemon with more than two split evos (Eevee and Tyrogue in generation 3). In this case, all evolutions will share the primary type of the original mon. If Eevee is Dragon/Fire, then all Eeveelutions will be part Dragon and not part Fire.
When Pokemon evolve, they turn into a mon that is a different species from its original evolution and shares one of the randomly chosen types within that seed. This means that evolutions target BST instead of typing, so that mons like Growlithe, who typically get weaker with evolution, have a chance to roll into much higher BST mons. So if Growlithe is Bug/Psychic, it will evolve into a pokemon who is at least part Bug or Psychic in the seed.
The Generation 1 trade evolutions (Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter) and Feebas evolve through happiness.
Scyther and Onix evolve with Metal Coat, and Poliwhirl and Slowpoke evolve with King's Rock.
Porygon evolves with Up-Grade, and Seadra evolves with Dragon Scale.
Clamperl evolves with either Deepsea Tooth or Deepsea Scale.
Item NPCs
The items normally found on the ground have been replaced by Item NPCs. Although there are only 56 NPCs scattered throughout the game, they will give you the option to pick one of three unique items, or a completely random one. This means that in general, your bag will have a better selection of items, even though you will have far less than Kaizo. The one exception is the NPC in Viridian Forest just before Pewter, who will give you a guarateed potion or status heal.
The Final Gym
The E4 is only a stepping stone in beating Paradox Kaizo. There is a final Gym, only known to trainers strong enough to defeat the Paradox Elite 4.
Other Rules
Limited Setup: You are only allowed to intentional increase attacking stats by one stage, whether through a move or item. If you've already used Bulk Up once, you cannot use it again as long as you have boosted attack. This also means that Swords Dance, Tail Glow, and Nasty Plot are outright banned.
Stronger Gym Leaders: All Gym Leaders have six pokemon.
More Free Dex Entries: The Deoxys in the Safari Zone has been replaced by a Shedinja, including a sign to give you the dex entry so you can check the type by the time you reach Fuschia.
Legendary Pivot: You may run Legendary or Mythical Pokemon from the Lab, but you must pivot before you do Koga. Pseudo-legendaries are still banned in the lab.
Legendary Friend: You may have a single 580 BST Legendary Pokemon as a favorite.
No Pickup: Pickup Items cannot be used.
Harder Sequence:
  • Misty must be fought immediately after Nugget Bridge.
  • Erik must be fought before Rocket Hideout.
  • Dojo must be cleared before Koga.
